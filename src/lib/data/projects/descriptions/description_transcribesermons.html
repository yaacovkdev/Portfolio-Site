We faced a major problem in our church, where some people who do not understand the native language in which the sermon was preached, and missed hearing the live translation, cannot rewatch the sermon uploaded on YouTube later. The solution to this problem was found through utilizing an increasingly available open source and low budget ASR and LLM models for people with consumer grade hardware. This audio library platform solved the problem my church has been facing by using Whisper with CTranslate2 interface. The collections are kept private, but public demo for sermon pages will be coming in the near weeks.
